THE REAL CAUSE OF THE CLOSE-UP FAILURES (2026-10-09)
- Planned to retrain the number reader. Before training, cut 362 text
  crops out of the real photos and scans and checked them by eye. The
  reader got about 9 in 10 real numbers right. It was not the main problem,
  and yesterday's note blaming it was wrong.
- The real cause: when the digits are very tall in the picture (a close-up,
  which is what the zoom and the framing box encourage), the step that
  FINDS the text cuts each row of digits into a top half and a bottom half,
  or finds nothing. The same picture shrunk reads perfectly: display_0305
  gives nothing at 960 px and "4000.00 / 57.68" at 0.99 at 480 px or less.
- Fix: a picture that gives no reading is read again at smaller sizes
  (settings.yaml: extra_ocr_sides, extra_whole_photo_sides). No training.
- Result, liters correct (before -> after):
      phone scans of 2026-10-08 (13):   5 ->  8
      real internet photos (23):        8 -> 10
      Large (140):                    129 -> 134
      generated close / car:           97 -> 99,  84 -> 87
      generated with ads:              79 -> 86,  77 -> 80
- WRONG VALUES: one new one. A phone scan with a glare spot on the last
  digit returns 57.60 for 57.68 as "not verified". The display has no price
  row, so nothing can check it, and every re-read gives the same 57.60.
  With the Saudi price range switched back on it would be refused. The old
  known case (15.15 for 15.16) is unchanged.
- A second new wrong value appeared during the work (an advert's "5.00"
  taken for the liters at the small size) and was fixed: a two-row result
  is refused if another size saw a different number under the same total.
- Training was not run. It can still help the last 1 in 10 (glare, widely
  spaced digits) once there are real pump scans to train and test on.
- 203 automatic tests pass.

ARAMCO DISPLAY LAYOUT (2026-10-11)
- A scan of an Aramco display (30.00 SAR, 13.76 L, 2.18 SAR/L in a small
  box beside the liters) was a retake although both big numbers were read
  right every time. Causes and fixes:
    * The phone number under the display (920012615) was taken for a row of
      the readout. Digits without a decimal point now count as a number only
      up to 6 digits.
    * The small "SAR/L" under the price box was attached to the liters next
      to it. A price label now goes to the number it is closest to.
    * A "SAR/L" from the grade list (GAS 91 2.18 SAR/L) was attached to the
      total on the same line. A label with its own number right beside it
      now stays with that number.
    * The same price shown twice (grade list and selected grade) counted as
      two candidates. It is one price.
    * New safeguard: liters x price = total also holds with the two swapped.
      If the "liters" is exactly a price from fuel_prices.yaml and the
      "price" is not, they are swapped back.
- That scan now returns 13.76, accepted. Today's 6 scans: 4 right (was 3),
  2 retakes, 0 wrong. No change on the other test sets except close-up
  with ads 86 -> 87. 205 automatic tests pass.

2026-10-04 (Day 1)
============================================================

WHAT WE DID
- Set up Python 3.11 and PaddleOCR on Windows.
- Built a script that reads a pump photo and prints the three values:
  Price (SAR), Liters, Price per liter.
- The app now crops the photo to the screen before reading it.
  Works for LCD screens and LED screens (red / green digits).
- Fixed the "988.99" problem: faint unlit segments on LCD screens were
  being read as digits. The app now reads each photo 3 ways and combines
  the results.
- Added the validation rules:
    * total has 2 decimals
    * price per liter must be in the price list (2.18, 2.33, 1.79)
    * liters x price per liter must match the total (within 0.02 SAR)
    * liters between 1 and 150
- Added a confidence rule: every value must be at least 0.95 confident,
  otherwise the result is "retake".
- Built an evaluation script that scores the app against a labels.csv.
- Built a phone scan server: open a page on the phone, take a photo of
  the pump, the result shows on the phone and in the PC terminal.
  (Development only: no login, no fraud checks yet.)
- Added a region of interest to the phone page: live camera with a green
  box, only the box is sent. Not yet tried on a real phone.
- Added a scan timer on the phone and in the terminal.
- Made scanning much faster: about 1.5 s per photo instead of 9 s or more
  (tens of seconds on large photos). The app now stops at the first good
  read, shrinks the screen crop before reading, and uses a faster CPU mode
  (needed PaddlePaddle 3.2.2 instead of 3.3.1).
- Phone page now freezes the picture on tap, so the phone can be lowered
  right away.
- Wrote 67 automatic tests. All pass.

RESULTS SO FAR
- 13 test photos: 11 accepted, 2 retake.
- No wrong value accepted on the 10 photos checked by eye.
- image.png: retake, confidence too low (0.908). Photo is very small.
- pump_0001.jpg: retake, price 1.99 is not in the price list.
